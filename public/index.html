How your data is handled

When signed in, memories and chat history are encrypted before database storage. The backend can decrypt them to answer questions. This is not end-to-end encryption.

Chat content enters your memory library only when you select Save to memory. Exports contain readable data. Provider backups may retain deleted records under their retention policy.

Guest memories and chat stay in this tab’s memory and clear on reload. They are not uploaded. Export your data to keep a copy.
